Which residues does the network look at?

Saliency, gradient × input, is concentrated in the first 25–30 residues and falls off past the cleavage sites. It says where the model looks, not why.

Gradient of the true-class score with respect to the one-hot input (same backward() as training, but with respect to input), times the input, averaged per class. Ask: compare with the hydropathy and charge profiles. The spike at position 50 is an artefact of reading left to right: the last residue enters the final state directly. A bidirectional RNN, as in TargetP, avoids it.
