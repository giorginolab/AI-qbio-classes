Where does a newly made protein go? Many carry an address label at the N-terminus.

SP

Signal peptide, about 15–35 residues. Into the secretory pathway: ER, Golgi, membrane or outside. All kingdoms of life.

mTP

Mitochondrial transit peptide, about 20–60 residues. Import into mitochondria. Eukaryotes only.

other

No N-terminal signal. The protein stays in the cytosol or goes to the nucleus.

Both signals are cut off after delivery and neither has a fixed sequence motif: they are recognised by their physico-chemical pattern along the chain. SignalP and TargetP predict them; TargetP 2.0 is a recurrent neural network (a bidirectional LSTM). Today we build a small version.
