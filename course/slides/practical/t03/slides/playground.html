Playground: with 300 sequences the GRU overfits

Training accuracy reaches 1.0, training loss below 0.01. The test loss is lowest at epoch 30, then noisier and higher; test accuracy plateaus at about 0.92.

gru_playground() trains a fresh GRU on a random subset of n_train training sequences; the test set is always the same. weight_decay is the L2 penalty of PyTorch (alpha in scikit-learn). Sliders with ipywidgets.
