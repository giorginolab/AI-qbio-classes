The loss notices overfitting before the accuracy does

hidden = 128: the test loss turns upward after epoch 10 (0.16 → 0.23) while the test accuracy barely moves (0.95 → 0.94). The network becomes over-confident.

Accuracy only asks whether the top class is right; the loss also asks how confident the network was, and punishes a confident wrong answer hard (minus log p of the true class). Ask: do test loss and test accuracy tell the same story? Which would you watch to stop training? Early stopping usually watches the validation loss. Lowest test loss: hidden 4 at epoch 15 (0.200); hidden 32 at epoch 9 (0.147, 0.149 at 15); hidden 128 at epoch 10 (0.163, 0.232 at 15).
