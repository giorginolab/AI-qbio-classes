Keep only cold-blooded animals and mammals above 10 kg, and fit one line.

Read naively: big animals burn more per gram than small ones.

The line joins the two bands and mixes size with body temperature. A trend across groups can differ from the trend within each group: Simpson’s paradox. Colour points by group before trusting a slope.
