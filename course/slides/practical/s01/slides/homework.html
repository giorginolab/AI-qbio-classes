Biological times are said to scale as mass to the 1/4. Do they?

  • For mammals, fit the exponent of gestation_days, female_maturity_days, litter_size and birth_mass_g against adult mass.
  • Which exponents are close to 1/4?
  • Pick one trait, list the species with the largest residuals, and propose an explanation.
Solution in the notebook: gestation 0.21, maturity 0.21, longevity 0.16, all with R² about 0.5; birth mass 0.91; litter size -0.10. Quarter-power scaling is a rule of thumb for times, not a precise law. Gestation residuals: look at marsupials.
