Exercise 3.1

Use the fitted law to predict

  1. the metabolic ratio of elephant and mouse, 204 000 times heavier;
  2. the metabolic rate of a 70 kg mammal. The table has a measured value for humans.
Answer: the law predicts a ratio of about 5 800 against the measured 8 600, right within a factor 1.5 starting from a factor 204 000. A 70 kg mammal should burn 58 W; a resting human burns 83 W, a bright light bulb.
