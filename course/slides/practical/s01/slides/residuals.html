The residuals bend: the law is a summary, not a truth.

Positive at both ends, negative in the middle. Fitted separately, small mammals give 0.60, medium 0.71, large 0.82: the exponent depends on which animals are in the table.

Much of the 2/3 against 3/4 debate comes from this curvature (Kolokotrones et al. 2010). A confidence interval answers how much a number would change with another sample, not whether the model is right.
