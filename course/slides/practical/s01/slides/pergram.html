Question

The exponent is smaller than 1. What does that mean for one gram of mouse and one gram of elephant?

And what would an exponent of exactly 1 mean?

With b = 1 every gram of tissue would burn the same power in every animal. With b ≈ 0.71 the power per gram falls as M^-0.29: big animals run slower per unit of mass. Heart rate, breathing rate and many other rates follow the same trend.
