All 627 species: R² falls from 0.95 to 0.82

There are two bands. One intercept per class, one common slope (0.70): R² back to 0.96. At equal mass a reptile burns about 6% of a mammal, an amphibian 3%, a bird 1.5 times.

Ask: what is the biology behind the two bands? Mammals and birds keep their body warm, reptiles and amphibians do not. The exponent is the same; what differs is the prefactor. One categorical variable fixes the fit.
