Two parameters, R² = 0.95. On the right axes 422 mammals over five orders of magnitude follow one power law.

The bootstrap says how precisely a number is known (±0.02). Only the residuals say whether the model is right.

Residuals are data. Bats and primates live several times longer than their size predicts.

Pooling groups that differ can hide a law or invent one.
