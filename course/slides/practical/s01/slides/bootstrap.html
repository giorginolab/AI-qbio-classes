Resample the 422 species with replacement, refit, repeat 2000 times. The interval excludes both 2/3 and 3/4: neither theory wins.

Ask: which theory wins? The bootstrap estimates how much the slope would change with another sample of species. It does not say whether a straight line is the right model; for that we look at the residuals.
