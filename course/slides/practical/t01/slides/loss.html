Fitting = minimising a loss

Each pair (a, b) has its own mean squared error. Linear regression finds the bottom with a formula; gradient descent walks downhill. Both end at a = 2.57, b = 0.70.

Neural networks (session 2) have no formula: they start somewhere and follow the slope of the loss in small steps. Gradient descent: a = 2.566, b = 0.702; LinearRegression: a = 2.567, b = 0.702.
