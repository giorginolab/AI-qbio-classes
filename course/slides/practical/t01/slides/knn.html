Underfitting and overfitting in one picture

Training error keeps falling as k gets smaller; held-out error is lowest near k = 10 (RMSE ≈ 0.89), then rises. The gap is memorisation.

Ask: why is the training error at k = 1 not zero? Some chemicals share identical descriptors but have different LC50, so the model cannot tell them apart. For k ≥ 100 both errors are high and almost equal: underfitting.
