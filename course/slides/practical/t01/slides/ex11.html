  1. Which descriptors are counts (integer values only)?
  2. What is the LC50 of the most toxic chemical, and in which row?
  3. How many chemicals have LC50 above 6, i.e. lethal below 1 µmol/L?

And: how much lethal concentration separates LC50 = 3 from LC50 = 6?

Answers: NdsCH and NdssC are the counts (5 and 7 distinct values). Max LC50 9.612 in row 259. 87 chemicals above 6. The scale is logarithmic: 3 units = a factor 10^3, so 1000 times less substance is needed.
