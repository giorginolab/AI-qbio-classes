Playground: overfitting you can see

20 noisy points of a known dose-response curve, polynomials of degree 0 – 20, optional ridge penalty alpha. Faint curves: the same model on 20 other samples, i.e. its variance.

poly_playground(): left, data, true curve, fit and refits on other samples; right, training and test error per degree, dotted line = noise level. Degree 15 shown: training RMSE 0.13 below the noise, test 0.8. Sliders work with ipywidgets.
