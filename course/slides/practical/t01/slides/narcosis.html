Question

Why should lipophilicity predict toxicity to fish?

And what are the points above the main cloud?

Most industrial chemicals act by baseline toxicity (narcosis): they dissolve in cell membranes and disturb them non-specifically. The more lipophilic, the more they accumulate in membranes and cross the gills, so less is needed to kill. Chemicals with a specific mode of action (e.g. pesticides hitting an enzyme) are more toxic than their logP predicts: the points above the cloud.
