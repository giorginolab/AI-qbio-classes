Exercises 5.1 and 5.2 · kNN

  1. Add kNN without the StandardScaler. What happens, and why?
  2. Try n_neighbors = 1, 3, 10, 30, 100 for the scaled kNN. What happens with very small and very large k?
5.1: unscaled R² drops from 0.62 to 0.57: distances are dominated by the descriptors with the largest numeric range, so the nearest chemicals are not the most similar. 5.2: k = 1 0.41, 3 0.58, 10 0.62, 30 0.60, 100 0.52. k = 1 copies the nearest chemical including its noise (overfitting); very large k flattens towards the mean (underfitting).
