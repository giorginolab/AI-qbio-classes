Exercise 5.3 · playground

  1. With 20 points, which degrees underfit and which overfit?
  2. Degree 15 with more data: n_train = 60, then 200.
  3. Degree 15, 20 points, with alpha = 1e-4, 1e-2, 1. And alpha = 100?
  4. Noise 0.05, then 0.5. Does the best degree change?
1. Degrees 0-3 underfit (bias); 4-5 best (test RMSE ≈ 0.24, noise 0.2); from 7 the test error climbs; degree 15 fits the noise (train 0.13, test 0.8), faint curves fan out at the edges. 2. 60 points: test ≈ 0.22; 200 points: ≈ 0.20, curves collapse on the truth. Overfitting depends on model and amount of data. 3. Any penalty removes the swings (≈ 0.27 for 1e-4 and 1e-2, ≈ 0.25 for alpha = 1, the best); alpha = 100 flattens to the mean (≈ 0.38), underfitting again. 4. Noise 0.05: best degree ≈ 7. Noise 0.5: best is a straight line. Noisier data support simpler models.
