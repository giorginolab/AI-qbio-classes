Linear

A straight line everywhere.

Random forest

A staircase. Flat beyond the data: it never predicts outside the training range.

MLP

Piecewise linear (ReLU). Outside the data it keeps going in whatever direction the last piece pointed.

No model can be trusted in the grey bands. QSAR guidelines call the region where a model is valid its applicability domain.

Both MLPs bend upwards at low MLOGP, predicting that very hydrophilic chemicals are more toxic: few training chemicals there, the curve is unconstrained, the result biologically implausible. The big unregularised network bends more and has small wiggles where it follows individual chemicals: overfitting, seen directly.
