Part A · a neural network for toxicity

A multi-layer perceptron is a stack of weighted sums and non-linearities

6 descriptors

32 hidden neurons, ReLU

LC50

Each neuron: h = ReLU(Σ w·x + b), with ReLU(z) = max(0, z). Weights start random and are adjusted by gradient descent to reduce the squared error. With enough neurons an MLP approximates any smooth function: its strength and its danger.

Test R² of this first MLP: 0.512. loss_curve_ stores the training loss after each epoch (pass over the data).
