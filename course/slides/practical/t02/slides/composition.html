Amino-acid composition: 20 numbers per protein

The fraction of each amino acid turns a sequence of any length into a fixed-size vector, and forgets the order completely. Enrichment per class on a log2 scale: +1 = twice as frequent.

All enrichments lie within ±0.25 on log2, under 20%. One has a story: Cys is enriched among transferases because about 15% of them transfer ubiquitin (EC 2.3.2.x, e.g. RING-type E3 ligases), whose zinc-binding domains contain twice the average Cys.
