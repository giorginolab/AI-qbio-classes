The forest drops most: it partly works as a “find the relative” lookup table.

For new enzymes, the family-split number is the honest one.

RF 0.40 to 0.31. LR and MLP drop less because they learnt less family-specific detail in the first place. The random-split number answers a different question: can I annotate a new member of a family I already know? For that, a BLAST search against annotated sequences would beat all of these models.
