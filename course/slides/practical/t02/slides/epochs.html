When should we have stopped?

Unregularised (512, 512): test MSE is lowest at epoch 109 (0.855), then climbs to 0.998 while train falls to 0.140. With alpha = 1 the test error flattens at 0.869 instead of rising.

Stopping at the minimum is early stopping. It must not be chosen on the test set: use a validation set carved out of the training data, MLPRegressor(early_stopping=True), as we do for the enzyme classifier.
