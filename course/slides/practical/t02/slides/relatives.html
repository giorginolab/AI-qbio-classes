Accuracy grows with the number of relatives in training

Random forest, random 5-fold CV: 0.33 with no relative in training (close to the family split, 0.31), 0.55 with 4 or more.

No relatives 0.325 (781 enzymes), 1: 0.351 (441), 2-3: 0.434 (429), 4+: 0.553 (396). The random-split accuracy is an average over these groups, so it depends on how many relatives the dataset happens to contain, not only on how good the model is.
