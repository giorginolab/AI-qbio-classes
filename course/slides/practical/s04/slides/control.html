Bigger models also give longer embeddings: 320, 480, 640 numbers.

Maybe the classifier just benefits from more columns. Design a control and run it. Hint: PCA can compress every embedding to the same number of columns.

Answer: compressed to 100 columns, families held out: 8M 0.534, 35M 0.567, 150M 0.537. The middle model is still best, by less, and the largest loses its advantage over the smallest. Part of what looked like a better model was a longer vector. Which model is best depends on a detail of the analysis: the differences between the two larger models are not solid.
