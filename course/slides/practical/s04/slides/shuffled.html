320 columns give many more ways to fit 2047 labels by chance.

Check it is not what happens: shuffle the labels and repeat the cross-validation for both representations.

Answer: both fall to chance, 0.151 (composition) and 0.154 (ESM-2). On the training data a 320-column classifier can fit many shuffled labels, but cross-validation is not fooled. The 0.65 is real.
