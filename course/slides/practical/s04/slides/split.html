Composition barely moves (0.291 to 0.270): it had little to lose. ESM-2 falls from 0.646 to 0.531: part of its score came from recognising relatives, and embeddings are very good at that. But most of the gain survives: for unseen families the embedding still doubles the accuracy of composition. It carries information about function that generalises across families.
