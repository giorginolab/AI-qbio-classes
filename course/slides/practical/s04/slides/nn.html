Exercise 3.1

Replace the logistic regression by a 1-nearest-neighbour classifier on the embeddings.

Each enzyme gets the class of the most similar enzyme in training. Compare the two splits. What does this model do, in biological terms?

Answer: 1-NN gets 0.698 with a random split, beating the logistic regression, and 0.541 with families held out. It is a search for the closest relative and a transfer of its annotation, which is how most proteins in databases are annotated in practice. It works as long as a relative exists.
