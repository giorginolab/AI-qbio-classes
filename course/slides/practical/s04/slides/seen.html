Question

We held the families out of the classifier. Were they held out of ESM-2?

No. ESM-2 was trained on essentially all known protein sequences, so it has almost certainly read these enzymes and their relatives. It never saw their EC class, so no label leaked. But the family is unseen only by the last, small, linear part of the pipeline. A protein from a family discovered tomorrow would be a harder test, which this dataset cannot run. As in session 2: unseen has degrees, and a score describes the population it was measured on.
