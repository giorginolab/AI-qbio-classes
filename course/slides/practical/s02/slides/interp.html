Interpolation

Inside the data, any reasonable model works and the data decide.

Extrapolation

Outside, the answer comes from the assumptions built into the model.

“Nothing new happens” for a forest, “continue straight in log-log space” for the law. The law extrapolates because its assumption is good physics, not because linear regression is a better algorithm.
