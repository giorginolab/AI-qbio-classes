Exercise 4.1 · look at the data first

Which eukaryotes have the most genes according to this table? Do you believe it?

Answer: the Iberian lynx with 4.7 million genes, an olive tree with 2 million. These are errors in the submitted annotations (probably features miscounted as genes), not biology. Bread wheat, a famously gene-rich genome, has about 110 000. Public databases contain such records and no model will warn you. We drop the 8 entries above 150 000 genes.
