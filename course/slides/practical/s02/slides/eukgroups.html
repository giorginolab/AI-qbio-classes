Reveal the table after students have tried. Fungi and protists, with small compact genomes, keep an exponent near 0.6 and a fairly tight relation. In animals the exponent is 0.2 and R² only 0.2: genome size says almost nothing about gene number. The proportionality law holds where genomes are compact and fades as non-coding DNA takes over.
