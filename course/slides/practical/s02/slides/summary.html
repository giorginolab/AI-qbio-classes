Inside the data, models agree. Line, kNN, forest and network all reach R² ≈ 0.95. Cross-validation cannot separate them.

Outside the data, assumptions decide. The law was right for the elephant and wrong for the human genome.

“Unseen” has degrees. Holding out whole orders cuts the forest from 0.80 to 0.48.

No score on your data tells you where the model stops being valid.

That takes knowledge of the system, or new data from outside. Session 3: protein sequences, where no two-parameter law exists and a neural network earns its place. Same questions: what is the simple baseline, and what does unseen mean?
