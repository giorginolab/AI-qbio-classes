Exercise 2.1

Do the reverse: train on mammals above 1 kg and predict the small ones.

Compare the linear model, kNN and the random forest.

Answer: R² below 1 kg: linear 0.54, kNN −2.71, random forest −2.11. Same picture: kNN and the forest fail completely, the line still explains about half of the variance. It is less good than before because the slope of large mammals (0.82) is too steep for small ones: the law is bent, and a straight line pays for it when it has to travel far.
