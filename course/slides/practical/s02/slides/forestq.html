Question

The law that predicted the elephant now says 2.3 million human genes. The forest says 11 000. Did the forest understand eukaryotes?

No. The forest does exactly what it did with the elephant: it repeats the largest value it has seen. This time “nothing new happens” is by chance closer to reality than “continue straight”, because gene number nearly stops growing. Neither model had any information about eukaryotes, and neither gave any sign of being outside its domain. Extrapolation succeeds when the assumption of the model matches the world, and you cannot check that from inside the training data.
