Question

What does “a species the model never saw” mean?

Tested on the Norway rat, the model has trained on the black rat and a hundred other rodents.

Species are related by evolution: close relatives have similar bodies and similar lifespans, for reasons our four features may not capture. The harder question: how well do we predict a kind of mammal entirely new to the model? GroupKFold keeps all species of the same order (rodents, bats, primates) together, either in training or in test.
