Hand-crafted or learned

Two ways to describe a protein

Hand-crafted features are designed by a person: amino-acid composition, length, hydrophobicity. Each column has a meaning, and nothing is learned.

Learned representations are produced by a neural network trained on a large dataset. The columns usually have no meaning we can name, but they can capture patterns nobody thought to encode.

The trade-off is interpretability against richness.

In the notebook: composition is 21 numbers (20 fractions plus length); column 0 is the fraction of alanine. The ESM-2 embedding is 320 numbers with no known meaning per column.
