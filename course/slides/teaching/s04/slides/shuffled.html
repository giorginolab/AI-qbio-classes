More columns, more ways to fit noise: check with shuffled labels

With 320 columns and 2047 proteins a linear model has many ways to fit labels by chance. A negative control tests whether that is what we are seeing.

Shuffle the labels, so that no real relation remains, and rerun the same cross-validation. Both representations fall to chance: 0.15 for each.

On training data the model would fit the shuffled labels; on held-out data it cannot. The 0.65 is real.

Exercise 2.1. Exact values: composition 0.151, ESM-2 (8M) 0.154 with shuffled labels. A shuffled-label control is cheap and applies to any supervised pipeline; it catches leakage and bugs as well as overfitting.
