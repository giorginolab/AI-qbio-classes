What “unseen” means

We held families out of the classifier, not out of ESM-2

ESM-2 was trained on essentially all known protein sequences, so it has almost certainly read these enzymes and their relatives. It never saw their EC class, so no label leaked.

But “family never seen” holds only for the last, small part of the pipeline. A protein from a family discovered tomorrow would be a harder test, and this dataset cannot run it.

“Unseen” has degrees, and a score describes the population it was measured on.

Whenever you use a pretrained model, ask what was in its pretraining data. For proteins this is nearly everything in UniProt; for text models it is a large part of the web. Benchmarks built from public data can overlap with pretraining data.
