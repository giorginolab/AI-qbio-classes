Nearest neighbours

1-nearest-neighbour in embedding space is annotation by homology

Give each test enzyme the class of the most similar training enzyme. Biologically, this is what annotation pipelines do: find the closest known relative and copy its label.

On the embeddings it reaches 0.70 with a random split, better than the linear probe, and drops to 0.54 with families held out.

It works as long as a relative exists. When none does, a model that has learned something general does at least as well.

Exercise 3.1. Exact values: 1-NN on ESM-2 (8M), random split 0.698, families held out 0.541. A nearest-neighbour baseline is a good habit whenever there is a notion of similarity: it tells you how much of a score is retrieval.
