The question

Can a model tell what an enzyme does from its sequence alone?

Enzymes are sorted by the reaction they catalyse into six EC classes. Random guessing among six balanced classes is right 17% of the time. Guess the accuracy of each representation before we start.

EC classes: oxidoreductases, transferases, hydrolases, lyases, isomerases, ligases. Collect guesses for composition and for ESM-2; come back to them at the result slide.
