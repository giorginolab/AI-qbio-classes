Confounders

A bigger model also gives a longer vector

The three models produce 320, 480 and 640 numbers. Maybe the classifier benefits from more columns, not from a better model. Two explanations, one observation: we need a control that separates them.

Compress every embedding to the same 100 columns with PCA (principal component analysis keeps the directions of largest variance). With families held out: 0.53, 0.57, 0.54.

The middle model still leads, by less; the largest loses its edge.

Exercise 4.1. When the ranking of models depends on a detail of the analysis, the differences between them are not solid. Good habit: whenever two things change together (model size and embedding length), design a comparison where only one changes.
