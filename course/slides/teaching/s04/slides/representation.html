Representation

A model only sees the numbers you give it

Every machine-learning model works on a table of numbers. Turning a protein, a molecule or an image into such a row is called choosing a representation (or features).

If the information needed for the task is not in those numbers, or is there only in a form the model cannot use, no amount of fitting will recover it.

Often the representation matters more than the choice of algorithm.

Link back to earlier sessions: in the QSAR or scaling sessions the representation was given (descriptors, body mass). Here we make it the variable of the experiment.
