Representation · composition

Composition treats a sequence as a bag of residues

The simplest description of a peptide is how often each of the 20 amino acids occurs: 20 numbers, whatever the length. Any classifier can use them.

But composition is order-free: shuffle the residues and the 20 numbers do not change. A hydrophobic stretch at positions 5–20 and the same residues scattered along the chain look identical.

The only way to inject position is by hand, by choosing which window to count.

Bag-of-words in text analysis is the same idea and has the same blind spot: “dog bites man” and “man bites dog” have the same word counts. Composition baselines are still worth running: they are fast and tell you how much of the signal needs no order at all.
