The receptors that read these labels recognise shape and charge, not an exact string. That is why simple string matching fails and a learned model helps.

Signal peptides are recognised co-translationally by the signal recognition particle (SRP) and threaded through the Sec61 translocon (SecYEG in bacteria). Transit peptides fold into an amphipathic helix, one face positive and one hydrophobic, recognised by the TOM/TIM import machinery. Boxes are schematic, not to scale. This is what SignalP and TargetP predict; TargetP 2.0 is a bidirectional LSTM, a recurrent network.
