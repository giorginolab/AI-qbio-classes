Interpretation · reading curves

An RNN gives a prediction after every residue, so we can watch it decide

Apply the final layer to h_1, h_2, …, h_50 instead of only h_50: what would the network predict if the sequence stopped here?

The curve of the probability of the true class against residues read shows where the decision is made. We can then compare that place with what we know about the biology.

This is a property of reading left to right: the hidden state at position t only depends on residues 1 to t. Exercise 7.1 asks students to look at misclassified proteins and guess why; encourage them to look at the protein names.
