Charge tells transit peptides apart

Transit peptides are rich in positive residues (Arg, Lys) and almost free of negative ones (Asp, Glu) over the first ~30 positions. Signal peptides have a few positive charges at the very start.

Exercise 2.1 in the notebook. Ask students to describe each address label in words from the two plots before showing the answer: SP = positive n-region, hydrophobic h-region, cleavage around 20–25; mTP = positive, no negative, moderately hydrophobic, amphipathic helix; other = flat. The information sits in the first 20–30 residues and its position matters.
