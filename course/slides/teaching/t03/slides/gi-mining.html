The general idea

Look at the data before modelling: the signal is often visible by eye.

A few averaged profiles told us where the information is (the first 20–30 residues) and what form it takes (hydrophobic stretch, positive charge). This sets expectations for any model, and gives us something to check its behaviour against later.

Later, the network’s reading curves and saliency maps will be compared with these same profiles. A model that agrees with independent biological evidence is more convincing than one with only a high score.
