Balanced classes make accuracy easy to read: guessing gives one in three. Signal peptides are short and tightly distributed; transit peptides are longer and more variable.

Swiss-Prot proteins from human, Arabidopsis, yeast, E. coli K-12 and B. subtilis, with the annotated signal length. A fixed 25% test set (900 proteins) is kept for the whole session. Some transit peptides extend beyond the 50-residue window.
