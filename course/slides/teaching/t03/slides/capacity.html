Training · capacity and data

How many parameters can the data support?

More hidden units mean more capacity: the network can represent more complicated functions, and also memorise more noise. Whether it overfits depends on the ratio of parameters to training examples.

In the notebook, 4 hidden units already reach 0.933 on test; 128 units push training accuracy to 0.988 while test accuracy does not improve. Past a point, capacity buys memory, not understanding.

Exercise 5.1. Numbers: hidden=4, final train 0.944, test 0.933; hidden=128, final train 0.988, test 0.937, best test 0.952. Tools against overfitting: more data, fewer parameters, weight decay (an L2 penalty on the weights, the alpha of scikit-learn), and early stopping.
