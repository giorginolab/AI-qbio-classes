The hidden state is a learned map

Projected to two dimensions with PCA, the final states of the three classes occupy separate regions, so a linear layer can separate them. SP and mTP trajectories move fast while the signal is read, then stay put.

Some mixing between other and mTP, the pair the confusion matrix confuses. Every trajectory starts at the same point (nothing read). A general lesson: a deep network’s main job is to transform inputs into a space where the final decision is simple.
