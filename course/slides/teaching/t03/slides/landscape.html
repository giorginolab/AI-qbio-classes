Context · sequence models

Recurrent networks are one of three ways to read a sequence

Convolutional networks slide short learned filters along the sequence: good at local motifs, fast, with a fixed field of view.

Recurrent networks carry a state along the chain: any length, order-aware, but sequential and slower on long proteins. Bidirectional versions read both ways.

Transformers use attention: every position can look at every other. Pre-trained on millions of sequences they become protein language models.

Real tools (SignalP 6, TargetP 2, DeepLoc 2) use bidirectional LSTMs, attention, or protein language models: the same ideas, scaled up. Session 4 uses ESM-2 embeddings. The choice is a trade-off between data size, sequence length and compute; for short N-termini and a few thousand examples, a small RNN is enough.
