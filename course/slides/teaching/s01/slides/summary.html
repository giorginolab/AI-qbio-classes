Concepts of this session

Representation. Log axes turn multiplicative relations into straight lines.

Fitting. Least squares chooses parameters; R² says how much variation they explain.

Precision. The bootstrap estimates how much a fitted number would vary with another sample.

Correctness. Residuals, not intervals, reveal a wrong model shape.

Residuals as data. Deviations from a law point at biology, or at how data were collected.

Confounding. Groups that differ can hide a law or invent one.

Next session gives the same data to models with thousands of parameters and asks what they can do that the law cannot, and the reverse.
