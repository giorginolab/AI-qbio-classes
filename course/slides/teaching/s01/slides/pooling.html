All 627 species: R² drops to 0.82; one intercept per class brings it to 0.96

There are two bands: warm-blooded mammals and birds, cold-blooded reptiles and amphibians. The common slope is 0.70. At equal mass a reptile burns about 6% of what a mammal burns, an amphibian 3%.

Ask: what is the biology behind the two bands? Endothermy. The exponent is the same; the prefactor differs.
