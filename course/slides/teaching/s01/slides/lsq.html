Least squares

Fitting means choosing the parameters that make the errors smallest

For each species the residual is measured minus predicted. Least squares picks a and b that minimise the sum of squared residuals.

orange: residuals

navy line: the fitted model

Why squares: they penalise large errors more, make the solution unique and give a closed formula for a line. Squared errors are measured vertically, in log10 units here, so a residual of 0.3 means a factor of 2 whatever the size of the animal. Fitting on the linear scale would let the elephant dominate.
