Errors on a log scale

On a log scale, errors are factors

The law predicts the elephant/mouse ratio as about 5 800; the measurement says 8 600. Off by a factor of 1.5, starting from a factor of 204 000.

For a 70 kg mammal it predicts 58 W; a resting human uses 83 W. A residual of +0.3 in log10 means ten to the 0.3, about twice the prediction, wherever the species sits.

Always translate log-scale errors back to factors when talking to biologists. An RMSE of 0.1 in log10 means typical errors of about 26 percent.
