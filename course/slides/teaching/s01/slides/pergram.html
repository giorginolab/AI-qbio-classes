What an exponent below 1 means

Big animals run slower, gram for gram

If B = a · M^b, the power per gram is B / M = a · M^(b − 1). With b ≈ 0.71, it falls as M^(−0.29).

With b = 1 every gram of tissue would burn the same in every animal. Instead, a gram of mouse burns about 24 times more than a gram of elephant. Heart rate, breathing rate and many other rates show the same trend.

Good moment to connect the maths to physiology: a mouse heart beats several hundred times per minute, an elephant heart about 30. Do not quote numbers that are not in the notebook unless you know them well.
