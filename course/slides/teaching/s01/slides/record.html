How the data were collected

A residual can come from the measurement, not the biology

“Maximum longevity” is a record: the oldest individual ever observed. A record grows with the number of individuals looked at.

For humans that is billions of people with birth certificates; for most species, a few zoo animals. Part of the human residual is therefore a sampling effect. Before explaining a residual biologically, ask how the number was produced.

General lesson: every column has a data-generating process. Maximum values, detection limits, annotation effort (gene counts in session 2) and curation choices all leave fingerprints that a model will happily learn.
