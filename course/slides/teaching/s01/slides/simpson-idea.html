The general idea

Colour your points by group before trusting a slope

When groups differ in level, a pooled fit mixes the effect you want with the effect of group membership. Including the group in the model, or analysing groups separately, separates the two. The same trap appears in any observational data: species, hospitals, batches, labs, sequencing runs.

Connect forward: in session 2, related species (orders) and in t02 related proteins (families) are groups that make random cross-validation too optimistic. It is the same structure seen from the prediction side.
