R², the coefficient of determination

R² is the share of the variation the model explains

R² = 1 − (sum of squared residuals) / (sum of squared deviations from the mean). A model that always predicts the mean scores 0; a perfect model scores 1.

R² measures how tightly the points follow the model, on the data used to fit it. It does not say whether the model is the right shape, nor how well it predicts new data.

R² depends on the spread of the data: the same law over a narrow range of masses gives a lower R². Session 2 introduces cross-validated R², computed on data the model has not seen.
