What this session is for

A simple model, used to learn what any model does

Choose the right scale before fitting anything.

Fit a model, and say how well it fits.

Quantify how sure we are of a fitted number.

Read the residuals: where the model fails is where the biology is.

Beware of groups: pooling can create or hide a trend.

The model has two parameters, so everything is visible on one plot. In session 2 the same questions are asked of flexible models with thousands of parameters, where they are much harder to see. Opening hook from the notebook: an elephant is about 200 000 times heavier than a mouse; how many times more energy does it burn? Collect guesses.
