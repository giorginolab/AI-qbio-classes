Confounding · Simpson’s paradox

A trend across groups can differ from the trend within each group

Keep only cold-blooded animals and mammals above 10 kg, and fit one line: the slope is 1.11. Small animals are cold, large ones warm, so size and body temperature are confounded.

Schematic, not data. In the notebook the careless dataset has 83 species and a pooled slope of 1.11, which read naively says big animals burn more per gram than small ones, the opposite of what happens within every group. A confounder is a variable that influences both the predictor and the outcome; here body temperature regime is associated with size in this subset and strongly affects metabolic rate.
