Models and parameters

A model is a rule with a few adjustable numbers

Here the rule is log B = log a + b · log M. Its parameters are a and b; the data decide their values.

Two parameters for 422 species is a strong compression. That is what makes the model useful: it is easy to interpret, it extrapolates in a predictable way, and it tells us when a species deviates from it.

Contrast for later: a random forest or a neural network has thousands of parameters and no single exponent to interpret. Session 2 compares them on the same data.
