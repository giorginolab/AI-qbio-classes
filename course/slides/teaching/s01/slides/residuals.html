Residual analysis

Residuals should look like noise; any pattern is information

If the model is right, residuals scatter randomly around zero, with no trend against the predictor and no structure by group.

A curve in the residuals means the shape is wrong. A trend in their spread means the errors are not constant. Residuals that differ by group point to a missing variable.

Standard plots: residuals against the predictor, against the fitted values, and coloured by group. They work the same way for any regression model, including neural networks.
