The general idea

A transformation can turn a curved problem into a linear one

Taking logs of both variables turned a power law into a straight line, and a straight line is the easiest model to fit and to interpret. Choosing the representation of the data is often the most important modelling decision, before any algorithm.

The same theme returns in every session: features (descriptors, amino-acid composition, one-hot encodings, embeddings) are representations, and the right representation makes a simple model work.
