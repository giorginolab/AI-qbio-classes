Residuals as biology · Part 5

Lifespan has a trend, and a wide spread

1023 mammals: longevity ∝ M^0.159, R² = 0.50. Half of the variation is not explained by size. The model is the same for every species, so each residual measures how unusual a species is for its size.

A low R² does not make the model useless: the trend is real, and the residuals become the object of study.
