Power laws and allometry

A straight line on log-log axes is a power law

If log B = log a + b · log M, then B = a · M^b. The slope b is the scaling exponent: multiplying the mass by 10 multiplies B by 10^b.

Allometry is the study of how traits change with body size. b = 1 means proportional scaling (isometry); b < 1 means the trait grows more slowly than mass; b > 1, faster.

Examples worth mentioning: skeleton mass scales with b slightly above 1, brain mass well below 1, heart rate with a negative exponent. The prefactor a sets the level, the exponent sets the shape.
