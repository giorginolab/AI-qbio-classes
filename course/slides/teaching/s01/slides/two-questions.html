The general idea

“How precise is this number?” and “Is this the right model?” are different questions

A confidence interval answers the first: how much the estimate would move with another sample. It says nothing about the second. A wrong model can have a very narrow interval. To check the model, look at what it leaves unexplained.

This distinction is one of the most useful things to take away from the session. More data shrink the interval but do not fix a wrong model; they only make the misfit easier to see.
