A gradient, not a separation

PC1 and PC2 explain 27% and 25% of the variance. Toxic chemicals lie mostly on one side of PC1, which is mainly lipophilicity and complexity (MLOGP, CIC0). PCA is a first look, not a predictor.

Loadings on PC1: MLOGP 0.69, CIC0 0.51. PC2 contrasts GATS1i and CIC0 with SM1_Dz. Half the variance is off the map, and the directions of largest variance need not be those that matter for the target.
