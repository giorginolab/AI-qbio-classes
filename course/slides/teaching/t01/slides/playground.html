Overfitting you can see

A degree-15 polynomial on 20 noisy points: training error falls below the noise level and fits on other samples (faint curves) fan out. Degrees 4–5 are best, close to the noise floor.

Exercise 5.3: degrees 0–3 underfit; from about 7 the test error climbs; degree 15 has training RMSE 0.13, below the noise 0.2, and test RMSE 0.8. With 60 points it drops to about 0.22, with 200 to about 0.20. Ridge alpha = 1 gives about 0.25; alpha = 100 underfits (0.38). Noise 0.05: best degree about 7; noise 0.5: a straight line.
