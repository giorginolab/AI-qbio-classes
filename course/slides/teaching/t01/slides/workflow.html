The shape of today’s session

Every data-driven model in biology follows the same five steps

Look

Distributions, correlations, a map of the data.

Baseline

The score of a model that knows nothing.

Model

Fit a function from inputs to the target.

Validate

Score it on data it has never seen.

Interpret

Ask what it learned, and where it applies.

Use this slide as the map for the session; each later slide belongs to one of the five steps. Steps 1 and 2 are not optional: most of the biological story in this dataset is visible before any model, and a score means nothing without a baseline.
