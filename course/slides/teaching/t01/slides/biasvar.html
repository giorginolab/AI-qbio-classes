Bias and variance

Too simple misses the pattern; too flexible learns the noise

Underfitting (high bias): the model cannot represent the true relation. Training and test error are both high.

Overfitting (high variance): the model fits the noise of its training set. Training error is low, test error high, and another sample would give a very different model.

Every flexible model has a knob between the two: k, tree depth, polynomial degree, network size, regularisation.

The diagnostic is always the same: compare error on the fitted data with error on held-out data. A growing gap means memorising.
