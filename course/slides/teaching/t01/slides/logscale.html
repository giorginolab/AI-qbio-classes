Units

Toxicity lives on a log scale

LC50 is the concentration that kills half the test fish in 96 hours, reported as −log10 of the molar concentration. Larger means more toxic: less substance is needed.

One unit is a factor of ten. LC50 = 6 means 1 µmol/L; going from 3 to 6 means a thousand times less substance.

Many biological quantities span orders of magnitude; logs turn ratios into differences that a model can add up.

Notebook question: difference between LC50 = 3 and 6? A factor of 1000. On a log scale errors become relative rather than absolute.
