The general idea

A difference smaller than the noise is not a result

Fold to fold, R² moves by about ±0.05 for every model, as much as the gaps between linear regression (0.56), kNN (0.62) and the random forest (0.60). The only solid conclusion is that MLOGP alone (0.41) is worse. Before ranking models, measure how much the score moves with the data.

The most transferable lesson: ask for the spread, not only the mean.
