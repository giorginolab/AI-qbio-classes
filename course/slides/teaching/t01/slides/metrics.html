Scoring

A score means nothing without a baseline

The simplest model predicts the mean LC50 for every chemical. Its RMSE, 1.40, is the baseline.

RMSE, root mean squared error, is the typical error in LC50 units. R² is the fraction of variance explained: 0 is no better than the mean, 1 is perfect, and it can be negative.

A line on MLOGP alone: RMSE 1.20, R² 0.26 on the test set.

R² = 1 − MSE(model) / MSE(mean). Report both: RMSE in the units of the problem, R² to compare across datasets.
