Correlation

Pearson’s r measures how well two variables follow a straight line

r goes from −1 (a perfect falling line) through 0 (no linear trend) to +1 (a perfect rising line). r² is the fraction of variance that a straight line on the other variable explains.

It misses curved relations, is sensitive to outliers, and looks at one pair at a time. A correlation is a hint about which inputs matter, not evidence of a cause.

MLOGP has r = 0.65 with LC50, SM1_Dz 0.41, GATS1i −0.40. Note r² ≈ 0.42 for MLOGP, close to the cross-validated R² of a line on MLOGP alone (0.41).
