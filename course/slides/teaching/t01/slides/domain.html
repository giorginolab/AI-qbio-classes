Applicability domain

A model is only valid near the data it was trained on

The fish model learned from 908 chemicals in a particular region of descriptor space, for one species and one exposure time. A new chemical far from all of them, or a new species, is outside its domain.

On Daphnia magna (546 chemicals, 48 h) MLOGP is again the top descriptor (r = 0.54), but the task is harder: R² 0.46 linear, 0.51 random forest.
