In kNN the knob is k

Large k averages many chemicals and flattens everything: underfitting. Small k copies single neighbours with their noise: overfitting. Held-out error is lowest near k = 10 (RMSE ≈ 0.89).

Exercise 5.2: k = 1 gives R² 0.41, k = 10 0.62, k = 100 0.52. Training error at k = 1 is not zero because some chemicals share identical descriptors but different LC50.
