The general idea

Overfitting depends on the model, the amount of data and the noise

The flexible model that overfits 20 points is fine with 200. A penalty on large parameters (regularisation) turns flexibility down without changing the model. The noisier the measurements, the simpler the model they can support. Choose complexity for the data you have.
