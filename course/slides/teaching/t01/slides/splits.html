One test split is one noisy measurement

Repeating the linear-model split with 200 seeds gives R² from 0.41 to 0.69, mean 0.57. Our single split scored 0.44, in the worst 2%.

A conclusion from one split of 227 chemicals can be off by ±0.1 in R². The cross-validated 0.56 agrees with the average over many splits and is the number to report.
