Feature scaling

Distances only mean something when units are comparable

kNN finds neighbours by distance in descriptor space. A descriptor ranging over hundreds outweighs one ranging over 0–1 and decides who is “near”.

Standardising fixes this. Without it kNN drops from R² 0.62 to 0.57 on fish, and from 0.48 to 0.24 on Daphnia, where two descriptors are in the hundreds.

Trees split one variable at a time and ignore units.

Exercise 5.1. Any method built on distances or dot products (kNN, PCA, clustering, neural networks) needs scaled inputs.
