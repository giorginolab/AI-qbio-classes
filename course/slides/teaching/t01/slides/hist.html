Look first: 908 chemicals, six descriptors

Histograms show range, shape and oddities. Two descriptors are counts with a handful of values; LC50 reaches 9.6, and 87 chemicals have LC50 above 6.

Dataset: Cassotti et al. 2015, UCI; fathead minnow. Descriptors: CIC0 (information content), SM1_Dz (heavy atoms), GATS1i (ionisation autocorrelation), NdsCH and NdssC (unsaturated carbon counts), MLOGP (lipophilicity). Exercise 1.1: the counts are NdsCH and NdssC.
