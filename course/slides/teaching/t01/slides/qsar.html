Quantitative structure–activity relationships

QSAR: predict what a molecule does from numbers computed on its structure

Structure

Atoms and bonds of the molecule.

Descriptors

Numbers computed from the structure: size, lipophilicity, atom counts.

Model

Learned from molecules whose effect was measured.

Property

The predicted effect for a new molecule.

Testing every chemical on animals is slow, costly and ethically difficult, so regulators such as EU REACH accept QSAR predictions. The idea is general: a measured biological property can be learned from numerical descriptions of the objects that carry it.

Descriptors are computed, not measured, so a prediction is available before a molecule is synthesised. Later sessions apply the same scheme to proteins: descriptors become amino-acid composition, one-hot sequences or language-model embeddings.
