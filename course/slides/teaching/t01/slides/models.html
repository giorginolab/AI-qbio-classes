Three ways to learn a function

Each model assumes something different about the data

Linear regression

The target is a weighted sum of the inputs. Few parameters, easy to read, cannot bend.

k-nearest neighbours

Similar molecules have similar effects: average the k most similar training chemicals.

Random forest

Many decision trees, each grown on a resample of the data; the prediction is their average.

kNN and forests capture curves and interactions. Both predict by averaging training targets, so neither predicts outside the range it was trained on.

kNN has one knob, k; the forest has the number and depth of trees. Neural networks (session 2) are a fourth family, fitted by gradient descent.
