Standardised coefficients put inputs on one scale

With all six descriptors test R² rises from 0.26 to 0.44. On standardised inputs a coefficient is the change in LC50 per standard deviation: MLOGP and SM1_Dz raise predicted toxicity, GATS1i lowers it.

Correlated descriptors share the credit, so single coefficients should not be over-interpreted. Raw coefficients cannot be compared because units differ.
