Fitting means walking down a loss landscape

Each intercept a and slope b has a mean squared error. Linear regression finds the bottom of the valley with a formula; neural networks walk downhill in small steps along the slope, gradient descent. Both reach a = 2.57, b = 0.70.

The valley is long and tilted because a and b trade off (all MLOGP values are near 2). Gradient descent drops into the valley then crawls along it; with learning rate 0.15 it diverges (above about 0.14). Centring the input makes the valley round: one reason to standardise inputs of neural networks. Test loss follows training loss: a line has too few parameters to overfit.
