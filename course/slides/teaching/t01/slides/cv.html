Cross-validation uses every chemical once as test data

Split into 5 folds; train on 4, test on the fifth, rotate, average. The spread across folds shows how much a score depends on which chemicals were held out.

5-fold CV R²: MLOGP only 0.41 ± 0.05, linear 0.56 ± 0.04, kNN (k = 10, scaled) 0.62 ± 0.02, random forest 0.60 ± 0.03. A setting chosen by looking at test scores, such as k, must itself be validated, or the score is optimistic.
