The general idea

A strong predictor should make biological sense

Most industrial chemicals act by baseline toxicity (narcosis): they dissolve in cell membranes and disturb them, so the more lipophilic, the more accumulates. Chemicals with a specific mode of action sit above the trend. A mechanism explains the main trend and also tells you where the model will fail.

A correlation becomes trustworthy when a mechanism explains it. The outliers are not noise: they follow a different mechanism that six generic descriptors cannot capture.
