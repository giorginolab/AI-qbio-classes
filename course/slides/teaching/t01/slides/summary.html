Look before modelling. Distributions, correlations and a PCA map showed the main story, lipophilicity, before any model.

Fitting is minimising a loss, by formula or by gradient descent. Validation measures generalisation, against a baseline.

Cross-validate and report the spread. A single split is noisy; differences below the noise are not results.

Complexity must match data and noise. The gap between training and held-out error is the sign of overfitting.

Interpret with care. Importance describes the model, and a model holds only within its domain.
