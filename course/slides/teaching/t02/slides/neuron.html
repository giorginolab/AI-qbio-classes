A weighted sum, then a bend

Each input is multiplied by a weight, the products are added with a bias b, and the total z goes through a non-linear activation. Here ReLU: max(0, z).

h = ReLU(w1·x1 + w2·x2 + w3·x3 + b). Without the non-linearity, stacking neurons would only ever give a linear function: a sum of linear functions is linear. The bend is what lets a network represent curves. ReLU is the simplest bend: zero for negative input, identity for positive input.
