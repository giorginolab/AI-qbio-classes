Predicting a category, not a number

The label is one of a fixed set of classes. Here: the EC class of an enzyme, the chemistry of the reaction it catalyses.

ECClassReaction
1Oxidoreductasestransfer of electrons (redox)
2Transferasestransfer of a group
3Hydrolasescleavage by water
4Lyasescleavage without water or oxidation
5Isomerasesrearrangement within a molecule
6Ligasesjoining two molecules, using ATP
2047 enzymes from UniProt/Swiss-Prot (manually reviewed), from human, E. coli, yeast, Arabidopsis and B. subtilis; 350 per class, 297 ligases. Each enzyme also has its protein family. Classifiers output a probability per class and predict the most probable one.
