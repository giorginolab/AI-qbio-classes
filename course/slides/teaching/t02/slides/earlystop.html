Early stopping

Watch the error on unseen data, epoch by epoch

Unregularised, the test error is lowest near epoch 110 and then climbs, while training error falls to 0.14. Stop at the minimum, chosen on a validation set carved from the training data, never on the test set. With alpha = 1 the test error flattens instead of rising.

Unregularised (512, 512): best test MSE 0.855 at epoch 109; after 300 epochs train 0.140, test 0.998. alpha = 1: best 0.868 at epoch 286, test 0.869 after 300. Early stopping is a form of regularisation: it limits how far the weights can travel from their small random start.
