Looking inside

Each model draws a different kind of curve

Vary MLOGP, hold the other five descriptors at their median. Linear: a straight line. Forest: a staircase that goes flat beyond the data. MLP: piecewise linear, continuing straight out of the data in whatever direction the last piece pointed.

Ask students to describe each curve and what happens in the grey bands. Both MLPs bend upwards at low MLOGP, predicting that very hydrophilic chemicals are more toxic: few training chemicals there, the curve is unconstrained, and the result is biologically implausible. The big unregularised network has small wiggles where it follows individual chemicals: overfitting seen directly.
