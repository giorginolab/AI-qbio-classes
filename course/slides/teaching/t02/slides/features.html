Features

Most models need a fixed-size vector

Amino-acid composition is the simplest encoding: every protein becomes 20 numbers, plus log length. It is easy and robust.

It also forgets the order completely: a protein and its scrambled copy look identical. A model can only use what its features keep.

The sequence shown is an illustration, not a protein from the dataset. Other encodings keep more: dipeptide frequencies (400 features), one-hot encoding of each position (session 3), embeddings from protein language models (session 4).
