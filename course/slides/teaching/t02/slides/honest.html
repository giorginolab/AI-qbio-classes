Reading a result

A modest score, read honestly, is still information

0.31 against 0.17 says composition holds a real but weak signal about enzyme chemistry. That is a finding, not a failure.

Always ask three things: better than what baseline (chance, a simple model, a similarity search)? On which split? And what would the number mean for the use you have in mind?

Overclaiming usually comes from the wrong comparison or the wrong split, not from the wrong model. Dipeptides do not help (0.376 random, 0.283 family): more features is not more information.
