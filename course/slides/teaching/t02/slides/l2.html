Regularisation

Penalise large weights and the gap closes

L2 regularisation (weight decay) adds alpha · Σ w² to the error. Sharp bends need large weights, so the network is pushed towards smoother curves. With alpha = 1 training R² stays near 0.8 and the largest networks generalise best (test R² 0.56).

Exercise A.2. Width 512 with alpha = 1: train 0.773, test 0.559. The general principle: when a model can do too much, constrain it. Regularisation trades a little training fit for a smoother, more stable function. alpha itself is a hyperparameter, chosen on validation data, not on the test set.
