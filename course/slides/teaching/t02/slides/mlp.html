The multilayer perceptron

Layers of neurons make a flexible function

Every hidden neuron sees all inputs; the output adds up the hidden neurons with its own weights. Each neuron contributes one bend, so more neurons give more bends.

With enough hidden neurons an MLP can approximate any smooth function. That is its strength and its danger: it can also fit noise.

This network has 6·32 + 32 weights and biases into the hidden layer and 32 + 1 into the output: 257 parameters. Deeper networks stack several hidden layers; the same idea applies. The notebook’s first MLP reaches a test R² of 0.512 on one split.
