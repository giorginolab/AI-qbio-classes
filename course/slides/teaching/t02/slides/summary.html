Neurons are weighted sums with a bend; layers of them make flexible functions, trained by gradient descent.

Flexibility needs restraint: watch the gap between training and unseen data, and use more data, regularisation, early stopping.

Models extrapolate by assumption, not by biology. Know the applicability domain.

Features limit what any model can learn. Composition forgets order.

Related samples leak. Split by family, patient or species to measure what you claim.
