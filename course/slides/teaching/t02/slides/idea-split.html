The general idea

The split defines the question the score answers

A random split asks: can I annotate a new member of a family I already know? A family split asks: can I classify a kind of enzyme I have never seen? Both are legitimate, but they are different questions with different answers. Decide which one you care about, then split to match.

For the first question, a BLAST search against annotated sequences would beat all of these models. Similar choices elsewhere: split by patient, by hospital, by species, by time, by chemical scaffold.
