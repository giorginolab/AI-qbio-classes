The general idea

A model is only valid where it has seen data

Inside the training data a flexible model interpolates; outside it, it extrapolates by an assumption built into its form: flat for trees, straight for ReLU networks, a line for regression. None of these is biology. QSAR guidelines call the region where a model can be trusted its applicability domain; every predictive model has one, stated or not.

Good moment to ask: what would the applicability domain be for a model of drug toxicity trained on pesticides? Or a protein model trained on human proteins and applied to bacteria?
