Related proteins are not independent samples

Letters are families, numbers members. In the dataset: 1102 families, 409 with more than one member, at most 8 members per family. StratifiedGroupKFold puts all members of a family in the same fold and keeps class proportions. The same issue appears with patients (several samples per patient), time series, and spatial data: whenever samples are grouped, split by group.
