Two questions behind every model

Part A · regression

How flexible should it be?

A neural network can bend to fit almost anything, including noise. We see how it learns, when it starts memorising, and how to hold it back.

Part B · classification

What does the score measure?

Related proteins on both sides of a train/test split make a model look better than it is. The split defines the question the score answers.
