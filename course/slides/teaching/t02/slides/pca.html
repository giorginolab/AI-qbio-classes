Look before you model

In a PCA map the classes overlap completely

Principal component analysis projects the 20 compositions onto the two directions of largest variance. No class forms its own cloud. Expect a classifier to beat chance, but not by much.

Ask: how large are the differences, do the classes separate, what do you expect for a classifier? A PCA map shows only two directions; classes could still separate in others, so this is a hint, not a proof.
