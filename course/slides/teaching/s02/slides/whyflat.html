Why models differ outside

Each model extrapolates with an assumption it carries inside.

kNN and forests return averages of training values, so they never exceed the largest value they have seen. For them an elephant is a big rat, about 3 W.

A ReLU network becomes a straight line far from the data, in a direction no one chose.

The law continues straight in log-log space. It is still five times too low, because small mammals have a lower slope (0.63) than large ones, but it is right within an order of magnitude, 3.5 orders of magnitude away from its data.

The five-fold error of the law is the bend from session 1 again: a slope estimated on small animals does not hold for big ones. Being off by a factor 5 after extrapolating over a factor of several thousand in mass is still remarkable.
