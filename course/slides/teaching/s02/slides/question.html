The question of the day

Train every model only on mammals lighter than 1 kg. Which one predicts the elephant?

Inside the data, all models look alike. Outside, each one shows what it assumes.

Ask students to write a guess. Session 1 fitted a two-parameter law to 422 mammals; today the same data go to models that assume nothing about the shape of the relation: nearest neighbours, a random forest and a neural network. Plan: four models on one curve (20 min), predict the big ones (25), species are not independent (20), a law that breaks (25).
