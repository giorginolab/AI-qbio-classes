The general idea

Outside the data, the answer comes from the assumptions built into the model.

This built-in preference is called inductive bias: ‘nothing new happens’ for a forest, ‘continue straight in log-log space’ for the law. The law extrapolated well because its assumption is good physiology, not because linear regression is a better algorithm. Before trusting a prediction, ask whether the input looks like the training data.

Every model has an inductive bias; without one, no prediction beyond the data is possible at all. The point is to know which one you are using, and whether it matches the biology.
