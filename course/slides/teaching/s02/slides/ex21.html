Exercise 2.1 · the reverse

Train on mammals above 1 kg and predict the small ones.

Compare the linear model, kNN and the random forest. Which one fails, and why is the line worse than before?

Answer: same picture. kNN R² −2.71 and the forest −2.11 below 1 kg; the line still explains about half the variance (R² 0.54). It does worse than before because the slope of large mammals (0.82) is too steep for small ones: the law is bent, and a straight line pays for it when it travels far.
