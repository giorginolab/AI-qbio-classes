Interpolation and extrapolation

Interpolation predicts inside the range of the training data; extrapolation predicts outside it.

inside the data: interpolation

outside the data: extrapolation

━ law: continues the trend ━ kNN, forest: flat at the last value ━ networks: straight, in a direction set by chance

A sketch, not data: the real curves follow on the next slide. Inside the training range the points constrain every model. Outside, there are no points, so the prediction can only come from what the model assumes.
