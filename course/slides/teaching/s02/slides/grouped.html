Dark squares: test species. Light squares: training species. One fold shown; the groups rotate.

scikit-learn’s GroupKFold takes a group label per row (here the taxonomic order) and never splits a group between training and test. The same tool handles protein families, patients or donors. The grouping level is a choice: holding out families is a milder test than holding out orders.
