Exercise 3.1 · bats

Train the forest on all mammals except bats, then predict the 48 bats.

Are the predicted lifespans too long or too short, and by what factor? Connect with Part 5 of session 1.

Answer: too short by about 20% on average (geometric mean of predicted / true = 0.80). Bats live longer than other mammals of the same size and reproduction, and a model that has never seen a bat cannot know. In session 1 this was a residual; here it is an error on new data. The same fact seen from two sides.
