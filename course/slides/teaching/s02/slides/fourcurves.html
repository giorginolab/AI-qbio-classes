Four models, one curve

5-fold R²: linear 0.949, kNN 0.947, random forest 0.928, neural network 0.953. The differences are the size of the fold-to-fold spread. A line, a wobbly average, a staircase and a smooth bend: different characters, the same accuracy.

Ask: which model is best? Describe the four curves. They cannot be told apart. The flexible models wiggle around the law without improving on it.
