The general idea

‘Unseen’ has degrees. A score describes a population: ask which one.

Random cross-validation answers ‘how well for another species from groups I know?’; grouped cross-validation answers ‘how well for a group I have never seen?’. Neither number is wrong. Choose the split that matches how the model will be used, and report which one you used.

Published accuracies are often computed with random splits on related samples and then quoted as if they applied to new kinds of data. This is one of the most common sources of over-optimism in biological machine learning.
