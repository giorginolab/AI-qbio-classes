Non-independent data

Related species share traits, so a random split leaks information.

Most statistics assume independent samples. Species are not: close relatives inherit similar bodies and similar lifespans, for reasons the measured features may not capture.

With a random split, a model tested on the Norway rat has trained on the black rat and on a hundred other rodents. A flexible model can simply look up the relatives.

The same happens with homologous proteins, cells from one donor, or patients from one hospital.

Part 3 predicts maximum longevity from mass, age at maturity, gestation time and litter size: 765 mammals in 24 orders and 115 families. With four features R² rises to about 0.8, and the flexible models do slightly better than the line, under a random split. Homologous proteins come back in the enzyme practical (t02), where protein families are held out the same way.
