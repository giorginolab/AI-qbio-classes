A law for prokaryotes

In bacteria and archaea, gene number is almost proportional to genome size.

exponent of genome size

0.98

942

genes per Mb (median)

An exponent close to 1 means proportionality: about one gene per 1 000 bases. A prokaryotic genome is genes packed side by side.

Fitted law: genes = 985 · size^0.964. The mechanism is simple and explains the near-perfect fit: little non-coding DNA, so more DNA means more genes. Keep the mechanism in mind for the next slide.
