Domain of validity

A law is a hypothesis about mechanism, and holds only where the mechanism holds.

Prokaryotic genomes are mostly coding, so genes track genome size. Eukaryotic genomes are mostly introns, repeats and other non-coding DNA. A lungfish has 40 000 Mb, 13 times our genome, with about 30 000 genes: the C-value paradox.

Neither model signalled that it was outside its domain. Whether extrapolation works depends on whether the model’s assumption matches the world, and that cannot be checked from inside the training data.

The general lesson of the session: the law was right for the elephant and wrong for the human genome, for the same reason. Its assumption was imported from the data it was fitted on. Knowledge of the system, or new data from outside the training range, is the only check.
