k nearest neighbours

Predict by averaging the k most similar examples.

To predict a new species, kNN finds the k training species closest in body mass and returns the mean of their metabolic rates. There is no fitting step: the model is the stored data.

k sets the smoothness. With k = 1 the prediction copies one neighbour and follows every bit of noise; a large k averages over a wide range and blurs real trends. With several features, ‘closest’ depends on how each one is scaled.

The notebook uses k = 10. Keep in mind for Part 2: a kNN prediction is always an average of training values, so it can never leave the range of targets seen in training.
