Inside the data, models agree. Dense data pin down the curve; cross-validation cannot separate a law from a network.

Outside the data, assumptions decide. Averaging models go flat, networks follow their seed, a law follows its trend.

‘Unseen’ has degrees. Related samples leak information; grouped splits measure transfer to new groups.

No score computed on your data says where the model stops being valid. That takes knowledge of the system, or new data.
